About D'Blossom

Growing learners. Building character. Shaping leaders.

D'Blossom Model Private Schools is a private school in Obada Oko, Abeokuta, Ogun State, committed to quality education and responsible character formation.

Our History

Established in 2017

D'Blossom Model Private Schools has been in existence since 2017. From the beginning, the school has focused on providing a purposeful learning environment where children and young people can develop academically, socially and morally.

Our approach combines disciplined teaching, individual attention, strong values and opportunities for learners to develop confidence, responsibility, creativity and leadership.
