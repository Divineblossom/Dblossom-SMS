Academic Programme

From foundational learning to senior secondary preparation

D'Blossom supports learners through Early Years, Basic Education and Senior School with structured classroom instruction, regular assessment, practical learning and character development.

Early Years

Building early literacy, numeracy, communication, curiosity, confidence and positive habits.

Basic Education

Strengthening core subjects, study skills, critical thinking, discipline and collaborative learning.

Senior School

Focused preparation for senior secondary certification, tertiary education and future career pathways.

External Examinations

Preparing students for recognised examinations

Senior students receive preparation and guidance for major Nigerian examinations and admission pathways.

JAMB / UTME

Preparation for the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination and the transition to tertiary education.

NECO

Preparation for National Examinations Council senior secondary examinations and related assessments.

WAEC / WASSCE

Preparation for the West African Senior School Certificate Examination and strong final-year performance.

WAEC GCE

Guidance for candidates taking the General Certificate Examination administered by WAEC.
